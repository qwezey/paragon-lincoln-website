Construction conditions change quickly. Once concrete is placed, walls are closed, insulation is covered, and
finishes are installed, important construction components may no longer be visible.

## Why It Matters

A dated, indexed photographic record can help owners, developers, builders, and project teams when questions arise
long after work has been concealed — supporting warranty administration, dispute resolution, and historical project
records.

## Our Process

- Review available construction plans and project information
- Perform site visits at appropriate stages of construction
- Photograph critical components using standardized checklists
- Record measurements, observations, and construction conditions
- Organize and index photographs into a permanent project record

For homebuilders and developers, this process becomes more than quality assurance — it becomes a customer-confidence
tool that demonstrates critical construction stages were observed and documented.
