Not every project should be managed the same way. Traditional engineering and construction projects typically
benefit from a structured, phase-based approach, while technology and data projects often move faster under an
iterative model.

## Waterfall for Traditional Delivery

For engineering and construction projects, PARAGON typically utilizes a structured Waterfall-style delivery model
with defined design, procurement, construction, inspection, and closeout phases. This approach works well when
scope is well defined and sequencing is critical.

## Agile for Technology Projects

For technology, AI, data, and software projects, an Agile or Scrum methodology — iterative planning, sprint
management, backlog management, and incremental releases — allows teams to adapt as requirements evolve.

## When Hybrid Makes Sense

Many projects benefit from combining both approaches: a Waterfall structure for permitting and construction
milestones, paired with Agile sprints for a technology component such as a field-data platform or reporting
dashboard. Choosing the right method — or combination of methods — starts with understanding the project's scope,
stakeholders, and risk tolerance.
