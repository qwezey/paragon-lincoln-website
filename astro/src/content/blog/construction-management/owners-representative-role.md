For homebuilders and developers, construction management is not simply about observing construction activity. It is
about actively protecting the owner's interests throughout the life of the project.

## Six Areas of Focus

- **Quality** — Is work being performed in accordance with approved plans and specifications?
- **Cost** — Are expenditures, change orders, and pay applications being properly monitored?
- **Schedule** — Is construction progressing according to the established timeline?
- **Coordination** — Are contractors, consultants, and testing agencies properly aligned?
- **Documentation** — Are decisions, field conditions, and changes being recorded?
- **Risk** — Are emerging issues identified early enough to allow corrective action?

An effective owner's representative brings engineering knowledge, field experience, and a documentation-first
mindset to every stage of a residential or development project.
